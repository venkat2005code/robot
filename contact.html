schedule TIMELINE & DELIVERABLES

How long does a feasibility simulation take?

Standard feasibility analyses take 48 to 72 hours from receipt of sanitized CAD files. Our simulation engineers verify reach envelopes, calculate joint torque limits, estimate cycle times down to ±0.1s, and deliver an interactive 3D kinematic video walkthrough.

verified COMMISSIONING & HANDOFF

Do you provide turnkey FAT and on-site SAT?

Yes. We execute 100% turnkey handoffs. Every cell undergoes comprehensive Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) on our Auburn Hills or Stuttgart floor with client production engineers present, followed by teardown, freight, reassembly, and Site Acceptance Testing (SAT) on your line.

security INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Are CAD models and part geometries kept strictly confidential?

Yes. All uploads are secured via end-to-end 256-bit encryption and trigger an automated mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA). Nexus maintains ITAR compliance and zero-cloud air-gapped workstations for sensitive aerospace and defense geometries.

precision_manufacturing CUSTOM ROBOT COMPATIBILITY

Can Nexus integrate with our plant-mandated robotics standard?

Absolutely. While our simulation team may recommend optimal kinematics, we maintain certified partnership integrations across KUKA, FANUC, ABB, and Yaskawa, paired with either Rockwell Allen-Bradley or Siemens S7-1500 PLC architectures.

01 // OUR MISSION

Automation that behaves exactly as simulated

Nexus was founded by a group of controls engineers who were tired of robotic cells that worked in the demo and failed on the night shift. Every system we ship is validated as a digital twin first, then run through a full factory acceptance test in our own labs before it touches a customer floor.

We keep kinematic design, PLC programming, machine vision, safety engineering and commissioning in-house. One accountable team, one set of drawings, one deterministic result.

Four engineering principles behind every cell
